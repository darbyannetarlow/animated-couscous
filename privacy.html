Antirot Privacy Policy

Effective September 25, 2026

Antirot helps you manage time in selected apps and save things you want to revisit. Your sessions, intentions, notes, saved links, and saved media are stored on your device. Antirot does not require an account, and we do not operate a server that stores this content.

Screen Time access. If you grant permission, Antirot uses Apple’s Screen Time features to apply app limits and support your sessions. This activity is processed on your device; we do not receive a record of your app usage.

Links and purchases. When you open a saved link, your device connects to the destination site, which has its own privacy practices. Purchases are handled by Apple. We do not receive your payment card details.

Support. If you contact us, we receive the information you choose to send, such as your email address and message. We use it to respond to your request and retain it only as needed for support.

Storage and deletion. You can delete content you have saved in Antirot. Deleting the app removes its local data from your device. Copies you exported and device backups managed through Apple may remain separately under your control.

We do not sell your personal information or use your Antirot content for advertising. If our practices change, we will update this policy and its effective date.

For privacy questions or support, contact Antirotapp@gmail.com.
