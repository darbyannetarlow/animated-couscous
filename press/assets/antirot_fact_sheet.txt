ONE LINE
Antirot is an iPhone app that asks what you came for before it opens Instagram, TikTok, Pinterest, YouTube or Facebook, then keeps the things worth saving.

SHORT (ABOUT 75 WORDS)
Antirot is an iPhone app that puts a question between you and your social apps. When you open Instagram, TikTok, Pinterest, YouTube or Facebook, Antirot asks what you came for and how long you want to stay, then lets you in with a timer running. Links and screenshots worth keeping go into an archive, filed under the session that found them. When time is up, the door closes again, and everything stays on your phone.

LONG (ABOUT 150 WORDS)
Antirot is an iPhone app for people who open social media with a purpose and lose it somewhere in the feed. It locks Instagram, TikTok, Pinterest, YouTube and Facebook behind a simple prompt: what are you here for, and for how long? Set an intention, choose anywhere from 1 to 40 minutes, and the app opens with a countdown on the Lock Screen and in the Dynamic Island.

A reel, a link or a screenshot worth keeping can be shared to Antirot, where it's filed under the session that found it with its creator, caption and thumbnail. Tags and collections gather the best finds over time. When the timer ends, the apps lock again and Antirot shows what you set out to do and what you kept.

Antirot has no account and stores everything on your iPhone. It starts with a free trial, then costs $3.99 a month, or $49.99 once for Antirot Lifetime. It was designed and built by Darby [surname], an independent developer and roller skating creator.

PRIVACY
Nothing to sign up for. Antirot has no account and no Antirot server. Your sessions, notes, tags and saves are stored on your iPhone.
Locks without looking. Apple's Screen Time framework gives Antirot an anonymous reference to each app you choose, enough to lock and unlock it. Antirot never sees what you do inside those apps or how long you spend in them.
Previews, and nothing else. When you save a link, Antirot fetches a preview from that page, or from TikTok for TikTok links, so your archive shows a thumbnail and caption. Antirot sends the link itself and adds nothing about you.
