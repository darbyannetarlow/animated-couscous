Whenever no session is running, a shield sits over the five apps. Tap through and you land on the intention screen, set your reason and your time, and then you’re in. The habit still works. It just starts with a question now.

If you’d rather skip the shield, put the Antirot widget where the app icon used to be. Same spot, same thumb, and every visit starts with a why.

Privacy

Everything stays on your phone, with no account to make.

  • No account, no sign in

    Download it and start. There is nothing to create, confirm, or remember.

  • Your archive lives on your iPhone

    Sessions, saves, and notes are stored in a local database on your device. Antirot has no servers holding a copy.

  • Screen Time holds the door

    Antirot uses Apple’s Screen Time framework to shield apps. Apple built it so an app like Antirot never sees what you watch or who you follow.

Questions

The things people ask first.

Which apps does it work with?

Instagram, TikTok, YouTube, Pinterest, and Facebook. But truthfully, you can save photos, videos or links from anywhere across the internet, with notes, and they are still tag-gable. The only difference is those aren’t part of a ‘session’ with an intention and time limit like the supported platforms.

Which iPhones does it run on?

Any iPhone on iOS 18 or later. The Dynamic Island features need a phone that has one.

What happens when my time runs out?

The shield comes back and the session moves to its close out. If you need longer, add five minutes from the Dynamic Island or the app. Extending is always your call.

Can I still get in when I really need to?

Always. Antirot never locks you out. It asks for a reason and a length, then opens the door. This is more of mindful scrolling tool than one that enforces abstinence. It just asks you to be mindful about why and for how long you scroll.

What does it cost?

Free trial then $3.99 /month OR you can buy Antirot Lifetime for $49.99.

Me and everyone else are so sick of subscriptions. So try it for low cost, and then keep your archive forever.

Do my saves survive if a post gets deleted?

Antirot stores the preview image and your note on your phone, so the memory of it stays even if the link goes dead. Screenshots and screen recordings are yours outright.
